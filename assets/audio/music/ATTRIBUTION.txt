Tip for the Balatro feel: Balatro plays its OST at ~70% speed. Try howler `rate(0.85)` or
WebAudio playbackRate 0.8-0.9 (pitch drops too = woozier, more Balatro-like). On game over,
ramp playbackRate down toward 0.5 over ~3 s (Balatro does exactly this).
