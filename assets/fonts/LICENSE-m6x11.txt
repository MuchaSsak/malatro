Usage notes (from author):
  m6x11      -> use font-size 16, 32, 48 px ... (multiples of 16)   124 codepoints (basic Latin)
  m6x11plus  -> use font-size 18, 36, 54 px ... (multiples of 18)   226 codepoints (Latin-1 + extras, up to U+03BC)
  metrics: average width 6, ascender 11, descender 3, x-height 8
This is the font family Balatro uses (Balatro ships m6x11plus).
